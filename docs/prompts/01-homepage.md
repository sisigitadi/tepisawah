# Google Stitch Prompt 01 — Tepi Sawah Homepage Production

Use the uploaded existing Tepi Sawah pre-opening homepage as the visual and content source of truth. Evolve it into the production/running public homepage without changing the established brand identity.

Requirements:
- Preserve the Tepi Sawah visual language, existing verified location, existing menu/product information, typography direction, imagery direction, and natural Sundanese/rice-field restaurant atmosphere.
- Replace pre-opening messaging with production/opening messaging only when the source data confirms the restaurant is operating.
- Remove the internal workflow/demo area from the public production homepage.
- Include: hero, quick actions, about/concept, menu highlights, promotion area only when verified, QR ordering CTA, reservation CTA only when supported, gallery, location/map, contact/footer.
- Do not invent ratings, phone numbers, opening dates, promotions, awards, testimonials, operational claims, or other business facts.
- Provide desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts.
- Define component hierarchy and important loading/empty/error states.
- Keep the homepage informational and customer-facing. Do not expose POS, KDS, waiter, or admin functions.

Production mindset:
- Responsive 320px–1440px+.
- Accessible contrast and readable typography.
- Clear CTA hierarchy.
- Fast visual hierarchy and mobile usability.
- Production-ready component states rather than a static screenshot only.
