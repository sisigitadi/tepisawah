## Brand & Style
This design system captures the peaceful equilibrium between natural Sundanese agrarian heritage and contemporary hospitality. Rooted in the experience of dining alongside Ciperna's open paddy fields, the aesthetic blends an editorial culinary publication vibe with functional, warm, family-friendly utility. 

The emotional tone balances tranquility, culinary craftsmanship, and welcoming warmth. The stylistic direction merges Organic Tactile Warmth with Refined Editorial Minimalism. Surfaces evoke natural rice paper, steamed grains, and dark volcanic earth, framed by rich paddy greens and roasted bean tones. Contrast is preserved through sophisticated serif headings paired with clean, accessible system typography, ensuring the identity feels artisanal, rooted, and effortlessly modern rather than rustic or kitschy.

## Colors
The color hierarchy translates the ecosystem of agricultural life in West Java:
- **Primary (`#2E6B34` - Forest Green)**: Embodies lush rice crops and fresh mountain air; reserved for primary interactive states, major callouts, badges, and high-emphasis indicators.
- **Secondary (`#DDA15E` - Golden Accent)**: Represents ripening golden rice grain and afternoon sunlight; utilized for secondary CTAs, key accent lines, rating stars, and interactive highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#6F3F24` - Coffee Brown)**: Reflects on-site artisanal coffee roasting; anchors subtle borders, micro-copy, metadata, and structural outlines.
- **Neutral Base (`#FEFAE0` - Warm Cream)**: Forms the canvas of the digital experience, eliminating sterile white glare in favor of an organic, hospitable surface.

Specialized named colors provide depth:
- `deep-moss` (`#183A1D`) provides high-contrast nighttime and footer dominance, framing header navigation and modal shells.
- `roasted-earth` (`#3D1F10`) replaces standard gray/black text tokens to maintain rich warmth and legible clarity.
- `rice-paper` (`#F8F4DB`) acts as the secondary container surface for cards, promo backdrops, and soft content panels.
- `amber-warm` (`#F3C644`) is dedicated to high-energy promotional callouts, live table status indicators, and operational highlights.

## Typography
The typographic voice pairs the grand, editorial heritage of `Playfair Display` with the balanced, modern functionality of `Inter`. 

Headlines command emotional presence: display titles use Playfair Display’s high contrast to invoke magazine-style culinary travelogues. Italicized subheadings add an artisanal touch for coffee origin stories and farm-to-table narratives.

Functional UI text, technical metadata, menu descriptions, and booking flows leverage `Inter` for legibility across mobile devices. For operational items such as order receipts, table ticket numbers, price tallies, and kitchen timestamps, a monospaced numerical treatment enforces neat tabular alignment.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid 12-column responsive grid capped at a maximum width of 1280px (`max-w-7xl`), allowing ample visual breathing room reminiscent of panoramic agrarian landscapes.

- **Mobile (< 640px)**: 4-column structure with `1rem` outer canvas padding and `1rem` gutters. Content stacks linearly with interactive touch targets spaced at least `0.75rem` apart.
- **Tablet (640px – 1024px)**: 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters and margins. Food and coffee menus switch into two-column presentation cards.
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: Full 12-column presentation with `2rem` gutters and `3rem` section margins. Editorial sections leverage asymmetric column distributions (e.g., 7 columns for imagery and spatial ambiance, 5 columns for narrative tasting notes and reservation CTAs).

Vertical rhythm utilizes generous intervals: standard section blocks are separated by `space-xxl` (64px) up to 96px on wide viewports to cultivate a relaxed cadence.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth avoids artificial synthetic drop shadows. Instead, it relies on earthy tonal layering and ambient natural illumination:

- **Tonal Grounding**: Lower elevation is achieved by placing cards tinted with `rice-paper` (`#F8F4DB`) directly over the `warmcream` (`#FEFAE0`) root canvas, defined only by a fine 1px border of `coffeebrown` with 15% opacity (`rgba(111, 63, 36, 0.15)`).
- **Ambient Field Shadows**: Floating interactive elements use a customized botanical shadow tinted with deep moss: `0 8px 24px -6px rgba(24, 58, 29, 0.18)`. This mimics soft, diffused shade beneath tropical canopy structures.
- **Golden Glow Elevation**: Prominent promotional cards and active ordering elements generate a soft solar halo on hover: `0 0 24px rgba(221, 161, 94, 0.35)`.
- **Atmospheric Glass**: Floating navigation bars and modal overlays utilize translucent `deep-moss` tinted glass with backdrop blurring (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px); background-color: rgba(24, 58, 29, 0.90)`), framed by a hairline border of warm gold (`rgba(221, 161, 94, 0.25)`).

## Shapes
The visual geometry reflects modern architectural bamboo gazebos and soft organic earthenware. A roundedness factor of `2` provides smooth, welcoming curves that soften structural borders:

- Standard cards, interactive menu tiles, and modal dialogs adopt an approachable `1rem` (16px) radius, scaling up to `1.5rem` (24px) for prominent feature frames.
- Form inputs, interactive buttons, and table shells carry a `0.5rem` to `0.75rem` radius.
- Status chips, cuisine dietary indicators, coffee process tags, and floating counter bubbles utilize full pill shapes (`rounded-full`) to complement the natural aesthetic.
- Fine hairline borders (1px) rendered in muted earth tones maintain crisp definition between closely stacked sections without visual clutter.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Styled in Forest Green (`#2E6B34`) with Warm Cream (`#FEFAE0`) text. Transitions on hover to Deep Moss (`#183A1D`) with subtle elevation lift (`-1px` translateY) and a subtle forest tint glow. Finished with a 12px border radius and bold label styling.
- **Secondary CTA**: Harvest Gold (`#DDA15E`) background with Roasted Earth (`#3D1F10`) text, designed for secondary actions such as "Reserve a Gazebo" or "Coffee Catalog".
- **Ghost / Outlined**: Transparent base bordered with `coffeebrown` at 25% opacity; hover state fills with `rice-paper` and brightens text to primary green.

### Chips & Badges
- **Pills**: Completely rounded (`rounded-full`), padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`, font `eyebrow-accent`.
- **Category Chips**: Inactive chips sit on `rice-paper` with `coffeebrown` text; active chips feature solid Forest Green with Warm Cream text.
- **Micro Badges**: Live indicators (e.g., "Roasting Today", "Outdoor Gazebo Available") feature a glowing emerald pulsing indicator dot alongside uppercase text.

### Cards
- **Menu & Dish Cards**: Built upon `rice-paper` backings with a soft 1px border (`rgba(111, 63, 36, 0.12)`). Images feature a gentle 16px corner clip with subtle scale zoom on hover (`scale-105` over 400ms). Price tags are anchored in bold roasted earth text.
- **Gazebo / Facility Cards**: Include a warm overlay gradient from `deep-moss` to transparent, placing editorial white/cream serif text directly over architectural landscape photos.

### Inputs & Selection Controls
- **Input Fields**: Crisp Warm Cream backgrounds framed by a 1px border in `#6F3F24` (20% opacity). Active focus states transition the border to solid Forest Green with an accent ring of warm cream. Labels are uppercase with tracked letter-spacing.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Custom circular or soft-square boxes with a 2px Forest Green boundary, filled with solid Forest Green and an Amber Warm checkmark when selected.

### Lists & Tables
- **Menu Pricing Lists**: Dotted leader lines connecting dish titles to price points, with ingredients set in italicized `Inter` body-small.
- **Operational Data & POS Tables**: Light ivory headers with uppercase tracking, separated by fine `coffeebrown` hairline rules (`1px solid rgba(111, 63, 36, 0.1)`).

### Thermal Receipt / Ticket Drawer
- Custom mono-spaced visual block on stark white surface with dashed tear-off separators (`border-dashed`), displaying order breakdown, table identification (e.g., "Saung 04 - Sawah View"), and operational timestamps.
