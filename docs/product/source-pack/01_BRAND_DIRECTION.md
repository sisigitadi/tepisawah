# Tepi Sawah Resto & Cafe — Brand Direction v1

## Product identity
Nama: Tepi Sawah Resto & Cafe
Domain utama: https://tepisawah.id

Produk digital terdiri dari:
1. Public Restaurant Website
2. Customer QR Ordering
3. Restaurant POS
4. Kitchen Display System
5. Waiter Interface
6. Admin/Owner Interface

## Visual direction
Arah visual:
- Natural
- Hangat
- Premium tetapi tetap ramah
- Terinspirasi suasana sawah, saung, bambu, alam, dan kuliner Sunda
- Hindari tampilan POS yang terlalu dekoratif

## UI principles
- Public website: visual storytelling dan photography-first.
- Customer ordering: mobile-first, sangat sederhana.
- POS: operational-first, cepat dibaca, tombol besar.
- Kitchen: high visibility, minimum interaction.
- Waiter: fast actions, table-centric.
- Admin: information density lebih tinggi tetapi tetap bersih.

## Important
Warna final, font, logo treatment, photography style, dan token visual harus divalidasi melalui Google Stitch sebelum implementation.
