# BRD — Tepi Sawah Digital Restaurant Platform v1

## 1. Business objective
Membangun ekosistem digital untuk mendukung pemasaran restoran dan operasional pemesanan makanan dari meja sampai pesanan selesai.

## 2. Business problems
- Pemesanan manual dapat menimbulkan salah input.
- Informasi order perlu diteruskan secara jelas dari customer/waiter ke cashier dan kitchen.
- Status pesanan perlu terlihat oleh role yang relevan.
- Menu dan harga sebaiknya dikelola dari satu sumber data.
- Website publik dan operasional restoran perlu memiliki hubungan data yang konsisten.

## 3. MVP scope
### Public
- Homepage
- About
- Menu
- Promo
- Gallery
- Location
- Contact
- CTA ordering

### Customer
- Scan QR meja
- Table identification
- Browse menu
- Category
- Product detail
- Cart
- Submit order
- Order status
- Call waiter

### Waiter
- Table list
- Manual order
- View ready orders
- Mark served
- Call/request handling

### Cashier
- Incoming orders
- Review/confirm
- Edit/cancel according to permission
- Payment recording
- Receipt
- Table/order overview

### Kitchen
- Incoming tickets
- Accept/start preparing
- Mark ready
- View order details

### Admin/Owner
- Dashboard
- Menu/category management
- Table management
- Basic user/role management
- Basic reports

## 4. Out of scope for initial MVP
- Full accounting
- Complex inventory/recipe engine
- Multi-branch
- Loyalty program
- Advanced CRM
- AI forecasting
- Advanced reservation engine
- Automatic QRIS reconciliation

These can be added after operational validation.

## 5. Success criteria
- Customer can place an order from a table QR.
- Cashier can verify and route an order.
- Kitchen can process and mark ready.
- Waiter can see ready order and mark served.
- Owner/admin can manage menu and tables.
- Public website and ordering use consistent menu data.
